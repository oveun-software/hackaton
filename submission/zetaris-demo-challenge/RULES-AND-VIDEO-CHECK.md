## Team and content permission

The rules allow individual or team entries. A team nominates one representative, who must have every member's agreement to enter and receives any team prize. The published rules grant Zetaris use of the video, written statement and attribution in marketing/social material while the entrant retains ownership. This is a separate permission from registering for the hackathon or using Zetaris's image registry. The final entrant must review and accept those terms with the team's agreement.
